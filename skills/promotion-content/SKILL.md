# Promotion Content

Create a promotion package:

- Release email
- Guest-share guide
- LinkedIn launch post
- Newsletter blurb
- Short-form captions
- Quote-card line

Use placeholders for links. Do not invent metrics. Do not include private contact details or recording links.
