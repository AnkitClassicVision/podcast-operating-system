# Promotion Content Workflow

The promotion workflow creates a practical package for the host, guest, and social channels.

## Promotion package

- Release email to guest
- Guest-share guide
- LinkedIn launch post
- Newsletter blurb
- Short-form clip captions
- Optional quote-card line
- Publishing QA checklist

## Principles

- Make sharing easy for the guest.
- Give copy options, not obligations.
- Use placeholders for links.
- Never include private file paths, recording links, or contact details.
- Keep claims tied to what the episode actually says.

## Swappable prompts

Replace `prompt_packs/default/social_posts.md` with your own voice or platform strategy.
