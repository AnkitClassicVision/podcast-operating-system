# Guest Promotion Guide

Thank you for joining the podcast. Here are simple ways to share your episode.

## Easy share options
1. Repost the launch post.
2. Share the YouTube link with one sentence about why the topic matters.
3. Use one of the draft captions below.
4. Send the episode to one person who would find it useful.

## Draft caption
I joined {{podcast_name}} to talk about {{episode_topic}}. We covered {{specific_takeaway}}. Listen/watch here: {{episode_url}}

## Assets included
- Episode link
- Thumbnail
- Short clip
- Suggested caption
- Quote card if available
