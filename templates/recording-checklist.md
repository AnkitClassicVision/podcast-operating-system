# Recording Checklist

## Before recording
- Confirm guest/time/link.
- Confirm local disk space.
- Confirm microphone/camera.
- Confirm backup recording plan.
- Confirm episode brief is open.

## During recording
- Capture cold open candidate.
- Mark timestamps for clips.
- Ask for concrete examples.
- Repeat unclear names/terms.

## After recording
- Save raw recording.
- Export separate tracks if available.
- Save transcript.
- Update episode status to `recorded`.
